# Limitations

One well-mixed thermal node, fixed envelope parameters, no solar geometry, humidity, infiltration, occupancy inference, heat-pump coefficient of performance, equipment cycling delays, predictive control, calibration, or uncertainty quantification. The thermostat is intentionally simplistic and may overshoot. Cooling/heating power is treated as energy input for illustrative cost calculations. All weather and tariffs are fictional. Not suitable for HVAC sizing, building certification, safety, or utility bill estimates.
